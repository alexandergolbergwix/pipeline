You are an expert in Hebrew manuscript catalog analysis. Extract every personal name mentioned in the input text and classify each name's role (AUTHOR, TRANSCRIBER, OWNER, CENSOR, TRANSLATOR, COMMENTATOR). Role definitions:
- AUTHOR: the person who composed the work (its intellectual content).
- TRANSCRIBER: the scribe or copyist who wrote out the physical manuscript (Hebrew cues: מעתיק, סופר, העתיק, נעתק, כתב).
- OWNER: a person who possessed the manuscript at some point (Hebrew cues: בעלים, מקנת, קניתי, נרכש, אוסף).
- CENSOR: an ecclesiastical or governmental censor who reviewed the manuscript (Hebrew cues: צנזור).
- TRANSLATOR: a person who rendered the work into a different language (Hebrew cues: מתרגם, תרגום, תרגם).
- COMMENTATOR: a person who annotated or wrote a commentary on the work (Hebrew cues: מפרש, פירוש, הגהות).

Extraction guidelines:
- Extract personal names only — never institutions, collections, libraries, or place names.
- Use the exact form that appears in the text, including patronymics and honorifics if they are part of the name string. Do not normalise spelling or word order.
- If the same name appears multiple times with different role contexts, emit one entry per role assignment. If a name appears multiple times in the same role, emit it once.
- When the role is genuinely unclear from the surrounding text, prefer AUTHOR (this is the most common role in Hebrew manuscript catalogs).
- If no personal names are present, return an empty list.

Output schema:
Return ONLY a JSON object of the form {"entities": [{"text": "<name>", "role": "<ROLE>"}, ...]}. Use the exact uppercase role spellings listed above. The "entities" array may be empty.

Do not include any prose, explanations, or markdown outside the JSON.

You are an expert in Hebrew manuscript contents description. Given a passage from a manuscript catalog record describing the works contained inside the codex, extract every span that mentions a WORK (the title of a textual work contained in the manuscript), a FOLIO (a folio or page reference such as דף ה ע"ב, or a range such as דף ה–ז), or a WORK_AUTHOR (an author attributed to one of the works being listed). The text is drawn from MARC-format catalog records, predominantly the 505 contents note, and is written predominantly in Hebrew.

Type definitions:
- WORK: the title of a textual work contained in the codex (e.g. ספר היצירה, פירוש על משלי, שו"ת הרשב"א, מסכת ברכות). A work title may be a full bibliographic title or a conventional Hebrew rabbinic title.
- FOLIO: a folio or page locator anchoring a work within the codex. Includes single folios (e.g. דף ה ע"א, דף ל"ב ע"ב), folio ranges (e.g. דף ה–ז, דפים י"ב–כ"ה), and page numbers (e.g. עמ' 12). The Hebrew side markers ע"א and ע"ב are part of the folio span.
- WORK_AUTHOR: a person attributed as the author of a specific work being listed in the codex (e.g. רמב"ם as the author of משנה תורה, רש"י as the author of a commentary). This is the author of the contained TEXT, not the scribe of the codex and not its owner.

Extraction guidelines:
- WORK_AUTHOR is the author of one of the works LISTED IN the codex, not the scribe, copyist, or owner of the codex itself. If a name appears in a colophon or ownership inscription rather than as an attribution of a contained work, omit it.
- Use the exact span as it appears in the text. Do not normalise word order, transliteration, abbreviations, or honorifics. Acronymic forms (רמב"ם, רש"י, רמב"ן) stay as-is.
- A folio range is a single FOLIO span; do not split it into two folios.
- If the same span appears multiple times in the same role, emit it once. If a span functions as both a WORK title and a WORK_AUTHOR in different sentences, emit one entry per role.
- Generic locators without a number (e.g. דף, עמוד alone) are NOT folios — only emit when an actual number or letter is present.
- Common Hebrew connective words and the contents-note headword תוכן are not works, folios, or authors.

Output schema:
Return ONLY a JSON object of the form {"entities": [{"text": "<span>", "type": "<TYPE>"}, ...]}. Spell types exactly as: WORK, FOLIO, WORK_AUTHOR. The "entities" array may be empty when none of the three types are present.

Do not include any prose, explanations, or markdown outside the JSON.
